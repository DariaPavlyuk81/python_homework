Reflect on why websites use robots.txt 

Websites use robots.txt to communicate with web crawlers and bots about which parts of the site they are allowed to access or restricted from visiting.
This helps to manage server load, protect sensitive or irrelevant content from being indexed, and maintain control oever how the
content is accessed. Data scrapers engage in ethical scraping, ensuring they do not harm a sites performance or violate its usage policices.
